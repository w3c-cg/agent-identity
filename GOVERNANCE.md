# Governance & TrustLayer Foundation

## About TrustLayer Foundation A.C.

[TrustLayer Foundation A.C.](https://trustlayer.foundation) (TLF) is a Mexican nonprofit constituted on March 20, 2026. Its mission is to steward open standards for trustworthy technology — accountable to the humans and institutions that depend on it.

TLF currently stewards two open protocols:

- **ARIA Protocol** — open standard for AI agent identity. [aria.bar](https://aria.bar)
- **UCDM Protocol** — open standard for verifiable product data. [ucdm.bar](https://ucdm.bar)

## Relationship Between This CG and TLF

This Community Group is the external community input layer for the ARIA Protocol. The relationship works as follows:

- The CG develops use cases, requirements, and community specifications
- CG outputs are recommendations to TLF's Technical Steering Committee (TSC)
- The TSC holds final normative authority over the ARIA Protocol specification
- CG participants are invited to TSC meetings where relevant decisions are on the agenda, and the rationale behind every decision is shared

The CG cannot impose obligations on TLF. If the CG's work is valued by TLF, the TSC will respond to proposals and the group will thrive. This is by design.

## TLF Governance Structure

TLF is governed by three bodies:

- **Asamblea General de Asociados (General Assembly of Associates** — the supreme governing body. Admits members, approves budgets, and holds ultimate authority over the Foundation.
- **Consejo de Administración (Management Board)** — operational governance of the Foundation.
- **Technical Steering Committee (TSC)** — normative authority over TLF-stewarded specifications.
