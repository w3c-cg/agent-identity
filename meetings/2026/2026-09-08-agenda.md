## 4. Current Topics

### 4.1 What this group is, and who stewards what

- **Context:** Chair-led orientation. The Community Group exists to work on an open registry protocol for verifiable AI agent identity that functions across organizational boundaries.

  Offered up front for clarity: the ARIA Protocol is stewarded by TrustLayer Foundation A.C., a Mexican nonprofit, whose Technical Steering Committee holds normative authority over that specification. This Community Group is not subordinate to that structure and is not being asked to ratify anything. Where the group produces recommendations touching ARIA, they go to the TSC as input. Where the group wants to work on something else, that is the group's call.

- **Expected outcome:** Discussion only. Shared understanding of the group's remit and of the relationship between the group and TrustLayer Foundation.

### 4.2 ARIA Protocol — presentation

- **Context:** Presentation of the ARIA Protocol (https://aria.bar/spec under CC-BY-4.0), offered as background rather than as a proposal. ARIA combines DNS anchoring, W3C Decentralized Identifiers, W3C Verifiable Credentials, and post-quantum-native cryptography, with a lifecycle Trust Ledger and a receiver-side admission handshake — the Agent Trust Protocol: Declare → Evaluate → Admit.

- **Expected outcome:** Q&A. Informational only. No decision sought.

### 4.3 Wider landscape and scope

- **Context:** Brief note pointing participants toward existing work on agent identity elsewhere, so the group starts with somewhere to read rather than a blank page, and to identify adjacent work being developed elsewhere to find potential synergies

- **Expected outcome:** Reading pointers. Suggestions and edits.
