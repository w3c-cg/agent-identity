## 2. Review of Previous Meeting

Meeting of 17 November 2026. The minutes will be posted separately.

The meeting was an overview of the ARIA and [agent.bar](https://agent.bar) updates and of the hiring use case. The chair informed attendees that the first issuer built on ARIA is currently live at agent.bar for testing and invited them to try the platform.

Ivan Moreno noted that the ARIA AP SDK lets the receiver issue a signed receipt of each request, so that sender and receiver can each keep a record of the interaction, whether or not the agent was admitted.

The landscape document fields were approved. The document is ready for additions, and participants who wish to add an item can do so through a pull request in the [landscape folder](https://github.com/w3c-cg/agent-identity/tree/main/approved-docs/landscape). It is for visibility and to keep track of who is working on what.

The chair sent the updates by email on 19 November and added notes to the v0.2 document.
