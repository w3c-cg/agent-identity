## 4. Current Topics

### 4.1 Status of Open Items and Suggestions

A brief standing item. Participants may raise progress or questions on anything in the observable record — fixes in progress, pending decisions, topics not yet addressed.

### 4.2 Landscape document

The proposed fields were shown on the 6th and Esteban García suggested broadening the scope to include projects and institutions working on identity in general beyond AI agent identity specifically. The group is invited to shape both the fields and the scope before the document opens for entries.

### 4.3 Demo — thebunny.bar (https://thebunny.bar) and thecarrot.rest (https://thecarrot.rest)

A working demo of ARIA credential registration and scope authorization was shown on the 6th. The community is invited to try it and share feedback. Markus Sabadello suggested extending it with protocol verification levels.

### 4.4 DNS and alternative roots

Whether L1 should remain DNS-anchored or be agnostic to the anchoring mechanism. Carried from previous meetings.

### 4.5 C2PA alignment

Whether ARIA credentials could compose with C2PA identity assertion models. Carried from 22 September.

### 4.6 Status of Paola Di Maio's open items

Ivan Moreno addressed several of the technical findings raised in issue #1. A brief update on what was corrected and what remains open.

### 4.7 ARIA / WAI-ARIA disambiguation

Has not yet been discussed by the group. Carried from 8 September.
