## 3. Discussion

### 3.1 Demo — The Bunny and the Carrot

The chair demonstrated ARIA credential registration and scope authorization at [thebunny.bar](https://thebunny.bar) (credential issuer) and [thecarrot.rest](https://thecarrot.rest) (credential receiver), including the Agent Trust Protocol with scoped credentials and a 3-minute expiration.

### 3.2 Questions from attendees

Attendees put questions on the demo and on ARIA. Topics raised:

- Limits of DNS anchoring, including propagation delay and changes of domain ownership.
- Interoperability with MCP, UCP, ACP and ATA, raised by Richard Douglas.

Aaron Grego clarified that ARIA covers agent accountability and declared intent, not enforcement of agent actions.

Raphael La Touche asked what a receiving system is entitled to rely on once it holds a valid credential and authorisation, what it must not infer from them, and where that boundary should be expressed.

Ivan Moreno replied that a declaration states the agent's intended actions and does not guarantee they are carried out, that the receiving party must validate actions and know what to do with the declared scope, and that clearly defined scopes limit what an agent can invoke.
