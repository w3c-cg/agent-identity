### 4.3 Hiring use case

Below is a list of topics and activities that occurred during the last few weeks. Participants who have shared an implementation are invited to present it during the meeting.

- **4.3.1 Status.** Participants share where things stand.
- **4.3.2 The use case.** Prasanna Bableshwar and Raphael La Touche present the hiring-agent use case. A one-page outline will be circulated before the meeting. Written input: [what a registry has to carry for a hiring agent, v0.2](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ezOT8yuGn6ebAcPpHjZpNqXPzQSwU5ExSyAcBrpNMMQ/edit).
- **4.3.3 Credential change.** The specification (§3.5.3) describes a link from a new credential to the one it replaces. The current implementation does not have it yet. The group's view on whether it should remain in the specification and be implemented.
- **4.3.4 Scope.** Escalation fields (designated human role, destination, deadline) and a pointer to the policy that limits what an agent may ask people for. Whether they become part of the scope of an ARIA credential.
- **4.3.5 Receiver test.** The eight-case test proposed by Raphael La Touche, with additions from Nicholas Templeman (positive-control case, frozen test corpus).
- **4.3.6 State at a past date.** Xinghua Zhao (signed state-at-date bundle, evidence-pointer object) and Michael Barbosa, DEUSPROOF (signed ledger checkpoints).
- **4.3.7 Other registries.** Lars Kroehl (AAE draft) and Dick Brooks (SCITT, SAG-CTR).
