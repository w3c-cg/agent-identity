Participants may flag progress, corrections, or questions on anything in the observable record — fixes in progress, TLF pending decisions, written suggestions not yet addressed. Not a discussion item; two or three minutes to surface what needs surfacing.

### 4.2 Landscape document

A shared map of who is doing what in agent identity, held in the repository. Fields and process to be shaped by the group. Carried from 22 September.

### 4.3 DNS and alternative roots

Whether DNS is the right anchor for L1, or whether the spec should be agnostic to the anchoring mechanism. Esteban Garcia raised identity wallets as one alternative. Relevant to the protocol's long-term portability.

### 4.4 C2PA alignment

Esteban Garcia proposed exploring whether ARIA credentials could compose with C2PA identity assertion models. C2PA is backed by major industry players and represents a concrete interoperability path worth examining.

### 4.5 Authorization scope across organizations

Whether a universal authorization schema is practical given the heterogeneity of corporate authorization structures across jurisdictions. Raised by Esteban Garcia; also touches Parviz Shariff's ARF contribution and Evgenii Arsentev's sub-agent issue.

### 4.6 Identifier collisions that fail open

Nicholas Templeman submitted a written note to the list: what must a registry bind alongside an identifier so that a false match is impossible rather than merely unlikely? Kenne Ives replied with related work. Both notes are in the list archive. The group may choose to take this up or carry it forward.

### 4.7 ARIA / WAI-ARIA disambiguation

Carried from 8 September. Has not yet been discussed by the group.
