## 4. Current Topics

### 4.1 Landscape document — fields and process

- **Context:** Proposal for a shared map of current work on agent identity across the ecosystem, held in the group's repository: one short entry per effort, the same fields for each, every entry naming its own known gaps and recording how its claims were established rather than simply asserting them. ARIA appears as one entry on the same terms as any other. A draft field set and a submission form will be presented for the group to shape.
- **Expected outcome:** The group shapes the fields and the process. Decision, if the group is ready — see §3.

### 4.2 DNS and alternative roots in the agentic web

- **Context:** Raised at the inaugural meeting: whether DNS will continue to serve as an anchor for agent identity, and what parallel roots — on-chain registries among them — mean for interoperability between them. Adjacent work is under way elsewhere, including at the Linux Foundation and the IETF.
- **Expected outcome:** Discussion only.

### 4.3 Use cases and requirements document — opening entry

- **Context:** Proposal to open a use cases and requirements document, with change-of-control events as a first entry: what happens to an agent's credential when the people or the organization behind it change, between scheduled re-verification.
- **Expected outcome:** Discussion. Agreement in principle to open the document, if the group wants it.

### 4.4 ARIA / WAI-ARIA disambiguation

- **Context:** "ARIA" is an established name at W3C, referring to WAI-ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications). The protocol discussed in this group is unrelated, and the charter, repository and welcome materials carry a disambiguation note. The underlying question has not been put to the group. Options include retaining the name with disambiguation, adopting a distinguishing prefix, or another approach the group proposes. Carried forward from the inaugural agenda.
- **Expected outcome:** Discussion, and a sense of the group's preferred direction. May be referred to the mailing list if participants want more time.
